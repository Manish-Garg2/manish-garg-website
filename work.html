DONOR PROGRESSION

Understanding why first-time supporters stop moving forward.

CONTEXT Acquiring new supporters was costly, yet a steep, unexplained drop-off occurred immediately following the initial contribution.
SIGNAL Audited 55,621 supporter journeys across transaction recency, frequency, and contact cadence. Revealed that 78% of attrition happened within an unguided 90-day window.
DECISION Replaced generic broadcast appeals with personalized trigger-based stewardship, stabilizing repeat participation and protecting multi-year lifetime value.
