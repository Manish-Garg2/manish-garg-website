Methodology & Source Standards

What Qualifies as a GCC:

An entity owned or contracted by a company headquartered outside India that performs high-value engineering, product development, R&D, finance, or strategic operations for its own global organisation.

Disclosed Headcount vs. Planned Hires:

Figures reflect public management commitments and reported plans from official press releases or verified news outlets. Planned hiring targets are explicitly distinguished from current stationed headcount.

Source Verification Hierarchy:
  • Verified Primary: Stock exchange disclosures (NSE/BSE/SEC), official company newsrooms, and investor portals.
  • Verified News: Reputable national business press (Reuters, The Economic Times, The Hindu, Business Standard, TechCrunch).
  • Secondary Research: Validated industry association roundups and commercial leasing filings.
Automated Data Refresh Pipeline:

Repository data is processed via automated GitHub Actions twice daily (06:00 & 18:00 UTC). Incoming items are schema-validated, normalised, and deduplicated prior to commit.
