01 / PERSPECTIVE

My work begins with the business question, not the technology.

I combine analytical thinking with AI, data and decision modelling to help organisations see problems more clearly and act more confidently.

Most data initiatives get lost stockpiling dashboards and chasing tools. I start at the opposite end: what decision is actually on the table, what evidence would genuinely change someone's mind, and where does human judgement need to intervene?

APPROACH Problem Framing · Decision Modelling · Practical AI
02 / FOUNDATION

Deakin University and the discipline of evidence.

I studied Business Analytics at Deakin University, where I led capstone projects, built decision models, and explored practical AI systems.

That work cemented a conviction: the most valuable analytics connects evidence to action, not just data to dashboards. Theory only earns its keep when it survives contact with noisy data and organizational reality.

EDUCATION Deakin University — Business Analytics
03 / WHAT I ENJOY

The questions that don’t have an obvious answer.

I've always been more interested in why the number moved than the number itself.

I enjoy taking an ambiguous situation — margin erosion despite revenue growth, donor drop-offs, operational bottlenecks — and figuring out what is noise and what is signal. The hardest problems are rarely about missing numbers; they are about unexamined assumptions and making trade-offs explicit.

FOCUS AREAS Margin Diagnostics · Retention Pathways · Unstructured Data & NLP
04 / THE PRACTICE

An independent practice grounded in honesty.

I'm not building another agency. I'm building an independent consulting practice grounded in clear thinking, honest evidence, and the discipline to say what the numbers actually mean — and what they don't.

If the data doesn't support the hypothesis, I say so. If a simple rule of thumb beats a complex model, we use the simple rule. The goal is always clarity for the decision-maker.
